---
title: Enquiries
description: Read enquiries from your website and move them through their statuses.
---

An **enquiry** is a message a visitor sent from your website. Each has a **source** (a short label such as
`contact-page`), a **payload** with whatever the visitor submitted, a status, and a received time.

## Reading enquiries

The tab lists enquiries 25 at a time, newest first. Choose **View** on a row to expand its payload as JSON, and
**Load more** for older ones.

## Statuses

```text
 new ──► in progress ──► resolved ──► closed
  │            │             │
  └──► closed ◄┘             └──► in progress  (reopen)
```

| From | You can move it to |
|------|--------------------|
| New | In progress, Closed |
| In progress | Resolved, Closed |
| Resolved | Closed, In progress (**Reopen**) |
| Closed | Nothing; closed is final |

The buttons on each row show only the moves that are allowed. Any other move is refused by the server.

## Who can change status

Owners, Admins and Agents can read and update enquiries. Submitters cannot see them.
