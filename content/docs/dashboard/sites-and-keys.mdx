title: Sites and keys
description: Issue, edit, rotate and revoke the keys your website uses.
---

A **site key** lets one of your websites send submissions and enquiries to your tenant. It is create-only: it can
never read data.

## Issue a key

1. Open **Sites & keys** and choose **Issue key**.
2. **Allowed origins**: list the website addresses (origins) allowed to use the key, one per line, for example
   `https://www.example.com`.
3. **Forms**: tick the forms this key may submit to.
4. **Requests per minute**: optional. If left blank, the default applies (30 per minute per key).
5. Choose **Issue key**.

The key is shown **once**. Copy it now; it cannot be shown again.

<Callout type="warn">
Treat the key like a publishable identifier, not a secret password: it ends up in your website's code. Its power
is limited by the allowed origins, the allowed forms and the per-minute quota.
</Callout>

Your website sends the key in the `X-Site-Key` header. See [Website integration](/docs/website-integration).

## Manage keys

| Action | Effect |
|--------|--------|
| **Edit** | Change allowed origins, allowed forms and the quota. Keeps the same key. |
| **Rotate** | Issues a new key. The old key keeps working for a grace period (7 days by default) so you can deploy the new one without downtime. |
| **Revoke** | Stops the key immediately. Cannot be undone. |

A key's row shows its status, origins, forms, quota and when it was last used.

## Limits that protect you

- **Per key**: the quota you set.
- **Per tenant**: a combined cap across all your keys (300 per minute by default).
- **Size**: public requests are limited in body size and field count.

Requests over a limit are refused; ask your platform operator if you need higher limits.

## Permissions

Only **Owners** can issue, edit, rotate or revoke keys. Admins can view them.
