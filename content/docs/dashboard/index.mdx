The dashboard, at `/dashboard`, is where a tenant's team builds forms, works enquiries and manages the keys its
website uses.

## Signing in

You need three things from your platform operator:

- your **Tenant ID** (a long identifier made of letters, digits and hyphens),
- your **email**, and
- your **password**.

Wrong details show "Tenant ID, email or password is incorrect." Repeated attempts are rate limited; wait a
moment and try again. Your session renews itself while you work and lasts up to 30 days of activity.

<Callout type="info">
The Tenant ID is not the slug. Operators can copy it from the Superadmin tenant list.
</Callout>

## Tabs

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Overview** | Counts of new and in-progress enquiries, active forms and active site keys, plus the latest enquiries. |
| **[Enquiries](/docs/dashboard/enquiries)** | Read enquiries and move them through their statuses. |
| **[Forms](/docs/dashboard/forms)** | Build forms, view their structure and submissions. |
| **[Sites & keys](/docs/dashboard/sites-and-keys)** | Issue and manage the keys your website uses. |

## What your role allows

What you can do depends on your role. If a button is refused you will see "You don't have permission to do that."

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything: forms, enquiries and site keys. |
| **Admin** | Forms and enquiries; can view site keys but not change them. |
| **Agent** | View forms; work enquiries. No access to site keys. |
| **Submitter** | View forms only in this dashboard. |

See the full matrix under [Authentication](/docs/architecture/authentication#roles).
